Engramscope gate G3: can your browser fetch Engram rows fast enough?

This page fetches 96 small byte ranges (12,672 bytes) per phrase straight from the model files on Hugging Face, the way the app will. It does not run a model. It tries nine phrases whose rows have not been fetched before, cycling through three concurrency levels, then repeats the first phrase. The browser cache is off. It checks every byte against a SHA-256 computed in Python.

Press Run, wait about a minute, then press Copy results and paste the text into the chat.
