Longuet-Higgins's 1968 holophone: listening test

Three spoken sentences were stored in one holophone: a bank of 32,769 resonators. Storing changes only each resonator's gain, by the energy that rang in its band. No waveform and no timing are kept. Each sentence was then cued with its first quarter. The holophone's answer after the cue is clip 3. Clips 5 and 6 are Lashley-style lesions.

Speech: Open Speech Repository (Harvard sentences, OSR_us_000_0010). Model: the ideal holophone as formalised by Willshaw (1971, Ch. 3). Measured, 3 sentences: the recall matches its own sentence best for 3 of 3.
